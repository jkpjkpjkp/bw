this is the design doc of `profession` tree

homo sapien - chef -- French chef
                   -- Chinese chef
            - engineer -- architect
                       -- electrical engineer
            - scientist -- professor --- in Computer Science
                                     --- in Chemistry

                                     --- at MIT
                                     --- at CMU
                        -- researcher


note: profession is a tree, but a person can belong to different professions, forming DAG. 
note: `entrepreneur` is not regarded as 'profession'. see `creation` table


tables in duckdb:
       `profession`: CATEGORY - (estimated) practitioners alive - (estimated) practitioners all

       `subprofession`: category - subcategory

       `honor`: category - highest distinction - receivers alive - receivers all
           e.g. "Biologist - Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine"
       
       `influence`: category -   ## TODO: capture e.g. money, fans, management etc.
